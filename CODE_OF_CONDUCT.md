# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions
that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other public channels.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact:
`omondicedo@gmail.com`.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
